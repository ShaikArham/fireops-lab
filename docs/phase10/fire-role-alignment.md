## Recruiter-facing message

Position the candidate as an experienced BI/data operations professional moving into payments operations. Lead with reconciliation, discrepancy investigation, monitoring, documented controls and clear stakeholder updates. Use FireOps Lab as practical evidence of focused preparation, while stating clearly that it uses fictional data and simulates payment operations.

Do not say the candidate has handled live payment processing, AML investigations, fraud decisions or SARs. Do not present FireOps Lab as affiliated with or endorsed by Fire Financial Services Limited.
